# MaidMarian

![Marian, hooded, drawing a glowing red arrow on her bow under a full moon](images/maidmarian-hero.webp)

## A token seeded with real stocks

MaidMarian is a token seeded with real stocks, with ANT at its heart. It does not exist yet. Soon.

It will be launched on the Launchpad in the mode called [STOCKS are SEED](launchpad.md#mode-2-stocks-are-seed).

## The basket

ANT, and five or six more stocks.

The candidates: AMD, AMZN, MSFT, NVDA, MU, AVGO, META, GOOGL.

These are the candidates, not the final basket. ANT is in for sure. Five or six of the eight stocks above will join it, and the choice is not made yet.

Each one is a stock token: a real share, held on chain. When you buy MaidMarian, the curve buys the stocks of the basket with your USDG and keeps them. The token is backed by what the curve holds.

How a buy and a sale work on such a curve: [What changes for the buyer](launchpad.md#what-changes-for-the-buyer).

## The launch

MaidMarian launches when the ANT stock token trades on chain. Not before.

1,000,000,000 tokens, on one curve on the Launchpad. The price follows the tokens sold: it starts low and moves with every buy and every sale.

Why wait? For now MaidMarian is an intention: a token backed mainly by the ANT stock token. That token is not on the chain yet. If it comes, the curve can buy it and MaidMarian launches. If it does not, there is no launch. No date is promised.

What is a curve? A contract that sells the token and buys it back, at a price set by how many tokens are out. No order book, no market maker: until the bond, you buy from the curve and sell back to it, at any hour.

What a curve's page shows: [A curve](launchpad.md#a-curve).

## The bond

Every buy turns USDG into the stocks. The curve holds them. When the stocks are worth the target, the curve bonds.

**At the bond:** 75 % of the stocks open one locked pool per stock, each paired with MaidMarian. 25 % go to the protocol.

**On every trade:** a 0.75 % fee (30 % to the protocol, 70 % to the creator).

The details: [The bond](launchpad.md#the-bond) and [Fees](launchpad.md#fees) on the Launchpad page; the contracts, [every fee](safety.md#fees) and [the risks](safety.md#risks) on the Safety page.
